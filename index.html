Kuber Gmail (personal)

Kuber is a private, single-user tool. It runs only on its owner's own computer and reads the owner's own Gmail read-only to keep the owner's personal finance records up to date (for example, the balance shown in the owner's bank alert emails).

It is not a public service: there are no other users, no sign-ups, and no data leaves the owner's computer.
